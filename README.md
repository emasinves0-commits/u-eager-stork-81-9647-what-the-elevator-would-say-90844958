## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` writes the one-line entry (71 characters, within the 120 cap) to `/output/entry.txt` and validates it: non-blank, at most 120 characters. Deterministic — the same bytes on every run, no network, no input, no randomness.

## The line

The elevator knows its regulars by floor and by hour. Dave rides to Floor 12 at 2 AM again; his wife thinks he's at the gym. The elevator keeps receipts — and punctuates the reveal with its own voice: *Ding.*
